02 / THE PROJECT

A working archive, not a promise of permanence.

Keep the network
usable. Record the work.

Interesting cryptocurrency networks can outlast the services and documentation around them. ARQOP works to keep selected chains connected and understandable by operating infrastructure and preserving practical knowledge about each one.

This is a best-effort engineering project. There is no formal uptime commitment, token, or plan to issue a coin. Every chain is evaluated on its own history, current condition, and the effort needed to support it responsibly.
